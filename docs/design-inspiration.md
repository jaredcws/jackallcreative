## Design patterns to borrow conceptually

### Hero

- Short, concrete headline.
- One strong subheadline that connects AI strategy to creative execution.
- Two CTAs: “Schedule an AI Opportunity Call” and “Explore Services.”
- Background can combine a portfolio image with subtle abstract AI linework.

### Service navigation

- Use 5–6 clear service cards instead of the current long list.
- Each card should include outcome, deliverables, and best-fit client.

### Proof

- Blend portfolio images with measurable or trust-based statements.
- Keep local/regional project credibility visible.
- Use case-study cards with industry tags.

### AI credibility

- Avoid generic robot imagery.
- Use workflow maps, prompt-system diagrams, before/after process visuals, and screenshots/mockups.
- Explain “AI consulting” through business outcomes: fewer repeated tasks, faster content production, clearer systems, better use of existing tools.

### Visual style recommendation

- Keep the brand approachable and grounded.
- Use a premium but simple palette: charcoal, warm white, muted gray, electric blue or acid green accent.
- Use current photography as a differentiator; most AI consulting sites lack real-world visual proof.
- Introduce AI through overlays, grid systems, fine-line diagrams, and subtle animation rather than cliché futuristic imagery.
