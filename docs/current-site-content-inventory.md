## Current work-with-us content

Current primary message:

- Too many companies cannot hire in-house creative departments.
- Jackall Creative provides creative, affordable, necessary creative/marketing solutions that keep clients moving forward.

Current engagement models:

### Individual services

- For clients with one or two immediate needs.
- Examples include headshots, a website, or other services.
- Estimates and timelines help prioritize projects and plan future budgets.

### Extended services

- Broader access to Jackall Creative services.
- Process includes identifying a list of creative/marketing projects, prioritizing it, establishing timelines, and completing work alongside the client.
- Goal is a long-term professional relationship and deeper understanding of the client’s business.
- Minimum three-month commitment.
- Useful for new businesses and backlogged project lists.
- Current savings claim: a year with Extended Services provides `$77,754` in savings versus hiring an in-house Creative/Marketing Manager.
- Current lead magnet: annual cost comparison download.

## Recommended content reuse priorities

1. Keep the “jack of all trades” origin, but mature it into “AI-guided creative department for growing businesses.”
2. Preserve the affordability/in-house alternative message; make it more outcome-driven.
3. Keep photography and video as proof of creative depth, but avoid letting them overpower the AI consulting repositioning.
4. Convert scattered service lists into packages: AI Readiness, AI Workflow Consulting, AI Content Systems, Creative Department, Web/Cloudflare Builds, Visual Media.
5. Retain local/regional proof from architecture, construction, commercial real estate, and small business clients.
