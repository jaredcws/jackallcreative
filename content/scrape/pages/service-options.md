- Individual Services
- Are you a business that has one or two needs right now? Whether you just need to update company headshots, build a new website, or any of the many services we offer, we offer individual estimates for each of your projects. Providing estimates and individual timelines will help you prioritize projects and plan for future budget updates.
- Extended Services
- Our Extended service option levels up your access to our services. We will identify your list of creative/marketing projects, prioritize that list, establish a timeline for each project, and begin to execute as those we were working alongside you. Our lines of communication will be open as we begin to establish what we aim to be a long-term professional working relationship. During this time Jackall Creative will gain a better understanding of who your company is and what your company needs that might not be listed. Our goal is to enable you to apply your energy and time to your business as you choose, while we work on the creative/marketing projects in the background.
- Extended service options request a minimum three-month commitment from our clients. This can be especially effective if you are starting a new business or have a backlog of projects. During that time we will develop a portfolio unique to your business. Project prioritization will be established in a collaborative effort to ensure our teams knock out your projects as efficiently as possible. All of our services are available to you from business card design, portfolio development, and even website builds.
